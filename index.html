Welcome

My research interests lie in applied microeconometrics and its interplay with political economy and economic history. I also explore how cultural factors, such as ideology and linguistics, interact with above contexts.

My recent work uses Japanese authorities' responses to infectious mortality in colonial Taiwan as a quasi-experiment to study their institutional consequences.

I am currently a Master's student at both Northwestern University and the Chinese University of Hong Kong. Prior to this, I completed my undergraduate studies in Labor Economics at Renmin University of China.
